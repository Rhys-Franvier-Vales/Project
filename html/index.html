Established in 1973, Vidya Vikas Mandal's Shree Damodar College of Commerce & Economics – the flagship institution of Vidya Vikas Mandal, is recognized all over Goa as a premier institution of higher education. The College has UGC recognition and is permanently affiliated to Goa University.
